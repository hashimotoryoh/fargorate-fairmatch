FargoRate FairRace (the "App") is an individually developed web app that supports pool matches played on FargoRate. This policy sets out what information the App handles and how it is handled.

## Information we collect

The App does not ask you to register an email address or a password. The only information it handles is the following.

- **Name and FargoRate ID**: the name and the numeric FargoRate ID you enter to link your FargoRate
- **Player information**: your name, location, rating, and rating robustness, retrieved from an external service using the name and FargoRate ID you entered
- **Guest details**: the name and rating you enter when you start without a FargoRate ID
- **Match records**: the disciplines and scores you enter in the App

You do not enter the player information yourself. It is retrieved from the public lookup feature of FargoRate. The only thing the App sends to FargoRate is the name needed for the search. Your FargoRate ID is never sent; it is used only to narrow the search results down to your player information.

Guest details are the values you enter, taken as given. They are never sent to an external service and their contents are never verified. The name is optional; if you leave it out, no name is stored and the App simply shows "Guest Player" on screen.

## How we use it

The information is used only for the following purposes.

- Identifying the user
- Showing your rating and match results on screen
- Indicating the approximate difference in skill against your opponent

It is never used to deliver advertising, and it is never sold or supplied to third parties. Your information is not disclosed to third parties except in response to a disclosure request based on law.

## Where it is stored and for how long

Your authenticated state is encrypted and stored in a cookie in your browser. No list of users is kept on the server.

The cookie is deleted when you sign out. Clearing your browser cookies deletes it just the same. There is no contact point to request deletion, because deletion is entirely in your own hands.

## Effect on your rating

The App reads your rating from FargoRate, but it never sends match results to the official system. No matter how many matches you record in the App, your official rating does not change.

## External services

The App queries the following external service. It is read-only and never receives your match results.

- **FargoRate**: to obtain player information (name, location, rating, and robustness) from a name

The service is not queried when you start as a guest.

How that service handles information is governed by its own terms.

## Analytics

The App does not install any analytics tools and does not track your behavior.

## Revisions

This policy may be revised as needed. When a significant change is made, the last updated date on this page is revised as well.

## Governing language

This policy is provided in Japanese and in English. If there is any discrepancy in interpretation between the two, the Japanese version prevails.

## Contact

The App is open source, and anyone can review its source code. For questions about this policy, please use [the issues in the repository](https://github.com/hashimotoryoh/fargorate-fairrace/issues).
